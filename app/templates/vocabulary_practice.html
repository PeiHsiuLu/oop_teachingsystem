How to Play Vocabulary Practice

In this activity, you will complete a sentence by guessing the missing vocabulary word.

Example:
Sentence: I eat an a___e every day.
Answer: apple
  • The system chooses words based on your current CEFR level.
  • The missing word will show its first and last letters as hints.
  • Type the full missing word in the answer box.
  • After you submit, the system will show whether your answer is correct.
  • Whether you answer correctly or incorrectly, you will see the word details.
  • If you keep performing well, your CEFR level may increase automatically.
