How to Play Vocabulary Practice｜單字練習玩法

In this activity, you will complete a sentence by guessing the missing vocabulary word.
在這個練習中，你需要根據句子脈絡猜出空格中的英文單字。

The test sentence itself will remain in English because it is used to evaluate your vocabulary ability.
題目句子本身會維持英文，因為它是用來測試你的單字理解能力。
Example｜範例：
Sentence: I eat an a___e every day.
Answer: apple
  • The system chooses words based on your current CEFR level. 系統會根據你目前的 CEFR 等級出題。
  • The missing word will show its first and last letters as hints. 缺少的單字會顯示第一個與最後一個字母作為提示。
  • Type the full missing word in the answer box. 請在答案欄輸入完整的英文單字。
  • After you submit, the system will show whether your answer is correct. 送出後，系統會顯示你的答案是否正確。
  • Whether you answer correctly or incorrectly, you will see the word details. 不論答對或答錯，你都會看到單字資訊。
  • If you keep performing well, your CEFR level may increase automatically. 如果表現穩定進步，CEFR 等級可能會自動提升。
